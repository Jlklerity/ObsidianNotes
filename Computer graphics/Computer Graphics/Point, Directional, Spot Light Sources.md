> [!caution]
> **Point lighting** is **more expensive than Directional Lighting** since point lighting requires the dot product of each vertices/fragment’s light vector (L to surface) and normal to get the intensity of the light to emit on that triangle. Compared to Directional lighting that uses just a universal/global light vector to dot product with each vertices/fragment’s normal vector.
