

## Non-preemptive Scheduling

### - Only occurs when a process either terminates or goes into waiting state


## Preemptive Scheduling
### - Occurs during process termination and waiting state as well.
### - Occurs during hardware interrupt (a process moving from a waiting to ready state or from a running to ready state)

### Occurs during software-generated interrupt (system call) such as an I/O request to access a file on the hard disk
