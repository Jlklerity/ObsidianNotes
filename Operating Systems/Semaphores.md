

## Types of Semaphores

### -Counting/general semaphores
### -Binary semaphores


## They are just normal variables used to coordinate the activity of multiple processes in an computer system

### Used to enforce mutual exclusion, avoid [[race conditions]], and implement synchronizations between processes

### Semaphores require two atomic operations, wait (P) and signal (V)

Fun fact: In dutch
- **P** stands for **Proberen** (meaning "to test" or "to try").

- **V** stands for **Verhogen** (meaning "to increment")
