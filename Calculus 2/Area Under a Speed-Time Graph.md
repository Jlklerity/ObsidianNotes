

## Why we care about area under a curve

On a **speed-time** graph, the area under the graph is the **distance travelled**.

### Constant speed
Speed $\times$ time = a rectangle. Trivial.

### Realistic speed
A real car accelerates and decelerates, so the graph is a curve. The distance is still the area under it — but there is no rectangle formula for it.

> [!hint] This is the whole motivation for Riemann sums: approximate an awkward area with rectangles we *can* compute, then take $n \to \infty$.
