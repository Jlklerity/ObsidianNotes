

## The output types are different

| | Notation | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Indefinite** | $\int f(x)\, dx$ | a <font color="#c00000">family of functions</font> (hence $+C$) |
| **Definite** | $\int_a^b f(x)\, dx$ | a <font color="#c00000">number</font> |

> [!warning] If your answer to a definite integral still contains $x$ or $+C$, you have not finished — you have not substituted the limits.
