

## The procedure

1. Find **one** antiderivative $F$ of the integrand $f$ (use the table of antiderivatives).
2. Set $C = 0$ — you do not need it.
3. Evaluate $$\int_a^b f(x)\, dx = \Big[F(x)\Big]_a^b = F(b) - F(a)$$

> [!question] Why can we drop the $+C$?
> Carry it through: $\big(F(b)+C\big) - \big(F(a)+C\big)$. What is left?

> [!warning] Order matters
> It is $F(b) - F(a)$: **upper limit minus lower limit**. Reversing it flips the sign of your answer.
